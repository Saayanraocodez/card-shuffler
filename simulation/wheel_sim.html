      What the model assumes
      
    
      
      
      Slot choice exact
      Card i goes to empty[uniform(n)] with swap-remove, from ChaCha20 keyed by SHA-256 (rng.cpp, shuffler.ino). Fixed seeds use the key SHA-256("fixed-seed:N") and the shuffle counter, so the plan is the one test fixed N prints.
      
      
      Wheel moves firmware
      stepper_move() ramp: 5000 °/s², 20–360 °/s, 3200 steps per revolution, shortest path, rounded to whole steps. Homing at 90 °/s, then the slow re-approach.
      
      
      Firmware waits firmware
      250 ms after beam B clears (T_SEAT_MS), 40 ms before the beam E check, 30 ms per scan or search stop, 60 ms after each eject, 250 ms for each shutter move.
      
      
      Card speeds estimate
      0.30 m/s through the feeder and 0.50 m/s through the exit nip (docs/01 §1.6), 30 ms roller spin-up.
      
      
      Gravity docs/03
      A card slides the last 10.5 mm to the hub at 0.73 g. Over the open window it slides outward at 0.47 g. Any card more than 0.5° inside the window counts as over it.
      
      
      Calibration
      The index trips at the firmware's home angle (90°), and the entry trim is −0.3°.
      
      
      Not modelled
      Double feeds, jams, retries, static cling, battery sag, and what a dropped card does next. The simulation flags the drop and carries on as the firmware would.
