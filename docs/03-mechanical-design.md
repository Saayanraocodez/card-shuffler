## 3.1 Frame and layout

The wheel axis is horizontal (Y). Angles are measured in the vertical XZ
plane from +X (3 o'clock) counter-clockwise. Fin k is a radial plate whose
counter-clockwise face lies on the radial plane at (wheel angle + k · 6.667°);
slot k is between fin k and fin k+1.

| | mm |
|---|---|
| Footprint (base boxes) | 320 × 210 (fits 12 × 12 in with margin) |
| Height | ≈ 290 (base 45 + axis at 100 + hopper end 145) |
| Wheel outer diameter (fin tips / discs) | 155 |
| Shroud outer diameter | 183 |
| Axial width of the cage | 92 clear, 98 over the discs, 110 over the hub bosses |
| Mass, printed parts | ≈ 900 g [EST] (wheel ≈ 300 g) |

## 3.2 The wheel

| Feature | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Slots | 54 | 52-card deck + jokers; two slots stay empty |
| Hub tube outer radius (card stop) | 22 | smallest hub that keeps the hub-end slot ≥ 1.1 mm |
| Card seated: inner / outer edge | 22 / 85.5 | card 63.5 mm radial |
| Fin tips and disc radius | 77.5 | card overhangs 8 mm so the beams see its edge outside the discs |
| Fin thickness | 1.4 constant | slot at the hub 2.56 − 1.4 = 1.16 mm; at the tip 9.0 − 1.4 = 7.6 mm (funnel) |
| Fin windows | two 17 × 30 and 19 × 30 mm per fin | mass −55 %, inertia ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻³ kg·m² with cards |
| Discs | spoked, 3 mm, ring r 62–77.5, 6 spokes | |
| Hub bosses | r 15, 6 mm beyond each disc | M8 nuts + washers clamp here |
| Bore | 8.6 for an M8 threaded rod | the wheel is clamped between nyloc nuts |
| Index tab | on the +Y disc at 90°, r 72–84, 3 mm thick | passes through the index bracket's beam |

Cards lie on the CCW face of fin k at the entry (fin k at 46.667°) and on the
CW face of fin k+1 at the exit (fin k+1 at 208.333°); the CW face is offset
1.4 mm from the radial plane, which the exit path geometry includes.

Printing: axis vertical, one hub boss on the bed, 110 mm tall; the top disc
bridges the ≤ 7.6 mm slot widths; the fin windows bridge 17–19 mm inside a
1.4 mm wall (fine at 0.2 mm layers). PETG. About 24 h.

## 3.3 Retention: shroud and shutter

In the lower half of the wheel gravity pulls cards outward. A fixed shroud
(inner radius 88.5, 3 mm thick, 100 mm wide, two segments 172°–270° and
270°–368°) stops them after 1.5 mm of travel; the card's edge then slides
on smooth PETG as the wheel turns (about 1–2 m per shuffle per card). The
exit window (202°–214°) is closed during loading by a curved shutter
(r 92–95, 15° wide) sliding on the shroud between guide flanges, moved 14°
by an MG90S through an M2 pin in the shutter's tongue (pin radius 15 mm,
±50°). With the shutter open a card at 208° slides out under 0.47 g into
the exit nip.

## 3.4 Feeder (entry at 50°)

The v1 feeder turned through 90° so that cards travel along their 63.5 mm
edge: hopper interior 65 (radial) × 91 (axial), floor = the plane of the
entry fin, 50° from horizontal. The deck slides against the gate under
0.77 g, so the lid ballast matters only for the last cards.

| Feature | r (mm) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Deck plate tip | 82 | 4.5 mm outside the discs; the card slides off the plate onto the fin |
| Beam E (vertical through the plate) | 84.5 | a seated card (edge at 85.5) blocks it; a card one slot off does not |
| Entry nip axis (Ø16 rollers, 4 O-rings 10 × 3) | 94.5 | inner edge 1 mm outside the seated card's edge |
| Beam B (gate beam) | 105 | |
| Gate lip (inner face of the gate wall) | 109.5 | slotted clamp, feeler gauge, 0.45 mm nominal |
| Feed roller axis (Ø27, 4 O-rings 20 × 3.5) | 121 | crown 0.7 mm above the floor |
| Hopper interior | 109.5 – 174.5 | back wall to 177.5; lid hinge on the back wall |

The nip releases the card 9 mm before the hub; the card slides the rest on
the fin face (gravity 0.77 g along the fin) and stops on the hub tube. The
next card is pre-staged 10 mm past the gate as in v1 (the feed roller stops
when beam B clears). Gate geometry, double-feed prevention and thickness
range are as in v1: t_max < gap < 2 t_min, adjustable 0.30–0.60 mm.

## 3.6 Towers, drive, base

* Two towers (12 mm plates, 219 × 192 mm, printed flat) at |Y| = 58–70:
  608ZZ bearing pocket at the axis, foot to the base, crossbar for the
  shroud ends, an arm at 46.7° under the feeder deck (screws at r 100.5 and
  121.5) and an arm at 208.3° under the chute (screws at r 108 and 127).
  The +Y tower carries a hollow standoff with the NEMA 17 (coupler inside,
  four access windows) and the index bracket; the −Y tower has the shutter
  servo bracket and the pin slot.
* Drive: NEMA 17 → 5 → 8 mm flexible coupler → M8 threaded rod (cut to
  165 mm) → wheel clamped by two nyloc nuts against the hub bosses; the rod
  turns in the two bearings. Direct drive, 3200 µsteps per revolution,
  0.11° resolution, 0.15 mm at the rim.
* Base: front and rear boxes (195 × 210 × 45 and 125 × 210 × 45) with
  removable bottom covers, joined by the tower feet; battery and perfboard
  inside; panel on the front face.

## 3.8 Tolerances and adjustment points

| Fit | Nominal | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Wheel angular position at the entry | ±1 mm at the mouth (±0.7°) | firmware `cal home`, `cal entry`, `cal exit` (0.05° steps) |
| Slot mouth | 7.6 mm at the tip, 1.16 mm at the hub | none; the funnel self-guides |
| Card in the cage (axial) | 92 for 88.9 | none |
| Shroud to card edge | 3 mm (1.5 mm travel + print) | shim the shroud tabs if cards rub |
| Gate gap | 0.45 | feeler gauge (the critical adjustment) |
| Nip roller crowns | 0.5 mm above the plane | none; idler springs |
| Shutter travel | 14° | servo endpoints `cal shutter` |
| Beam thresholds | auto | `cal beams` |
| Bearing pockets | 22.2 for 608ZZ | press fit |
| Coupler / rod | M8 in an 8 mm coupler | the coupler grips the thread; use a rod with a filed flat if it slips |

Print variation is absorbed by the 3–7 mm slot mouth and by the firmware
trims; nothing that affects randomness relies on a printed dimension
except the fin pitch, which is a division of the circle and cannot drift.

## 3.9 Card protection

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Scratches | rolling contact in the nips; cards slide face-on over smooth fins and the gate lip is rounded; felt on the chute end wall and under the lid boss |
| Edge wear | the hub stop is a 2 mm PETG tube wall (compliant); edges ride on the shroud only in the lower half; a strip of self-adhesive felt on the shroud interior is an optional upgrade |
| Bent corners | straight radial entry into a funnel; the card is supported by the fin face throughout; the exit nip pulls along the same plane |
| Excessive pressure | nips ≈ 2 N; lid ≈ 0.6 N |
| Exposed faces | face down in the hopper under the lid; faces are between fins inside the cage; the chute shows only the top card's back |
| Double feed | gate at 1.3–1.5 t; B-beam block-time check; end count; two cards in one slot come out together (adjacent) and the count flags it |
| Cards flying out | shroud + shutter; in the upper half gravity seats them |
| Print variation | funnel mouths; firmware trims; feeler-gauge gate |

## 3.10 Things a builder may want to change

`r_hub` (smaller wheel, thinner hub slots), `entry_slot_ang` (steeper hopper
= shorter footprint, taller machine), `exit_slot_ang` (steeper exit = faster
slide-out, longer chute), `fin_t`, `n_slots` (the firmware constant
`N_SLOTS_HW` must match), `tower_x0/x1` for bigger beds.
