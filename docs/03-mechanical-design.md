## 3.1 Frame, layout and the clearance rule

The wheel axis is horizontal (Y). Angles are measured in the vertical XZ
plane from +X (3 o'clock), counter-clockwise. Fin k is a radial plate whose
counter-clockwise face lies on the radial plane at (wheel angle + k · 6.667°).
Slot k is the space between fin k and fin k+1.

**Clearance rule.** Whenever the wheel turns, the seated cards sweep every
point with radius ≤ 85.5 mm and |Y| ≤ 46 mm, including card play in the cage.
The discs sweep r ≤ 77.5 at |Y| 46–49, and the index tab sweeps r 66–76 at
Y 49–53. Nothing fixed may enter these volumes, with at least 0.4 mm to
spare. Every fixed part at the entry and exit therefore starts outside
r 86. Cards also carry play out of the lower half: on the shroud they sit
3 mm out (r 88.5). The ramp on the end of `shroud_B` (§3.3) takes that back
to 0.5 mm before the entry, so a counter-clockwise move never brings it to
the entry nip roller (nearest point r 88.3).

24 h, PETG. The top disc bridges the ≤ 7.6 mm slot widths.

## 3.3 Retention: shroud, ramp and shutter

In the lower half, gravity pulls cards outward. A fixed shroud stops them
after 3 mm of travel: inner radius 88.5, 3 mm thick, in two segments from
172° to 270° and from 270° to 368°. It runs from tower face to tower face
(116 mm), so each segment prints standing on a full edge. The −Y edge is
cut back to 92 mm wide where the shutter's drive tab travels (202°–236.5°).

**Re-seating ramp.** After leaving the shroud at 8°, a card is pulled back
to the hub only by gravity along the fin, and friction resists that. A
fast counter-clockwise move can bring it to the entry nip roller (42.2°,
r 88.3) still 3 mm out. `simulation/card_slip.js` runs whole shuffles with
the firmware's moves: cards reach the roller in every shuffle once
card-to-PETG friction is above about 0.37, which is a card that starts to
slide on PETG at about 20° of tilt. `shroud_B` therefore continues past 8° as a 3 mm
ramp whose inner radius falls from 88.5 to 86.0 by 26°, stays there to
29°, and flares to 87.5 at 32°, so cards arriving clockwise from the top
slide in under it. It ends about 3 mm short of the feeder bushing bracket
(`feeder_shroud` check) and 5° short of the roller. With the ramp, the most
play that reaches the roller at friction up to 0.6 is 0.5 mm (limit
2.8 mm), and the wheel keeps its shortest-path moves.
Across the exit window only a strap at |Y| 47–58 remains, outside the
card width. The card edge then slides
on smooth PETG as the wheel turns.

The exit window (202°–214°) is closed during loading by a **shutter blade
on the inside of the shroud**. The blade is 1.2 mm thick at r 87.0–88.2 and
spans 199.4°–216.6°. Its ends taper to 0.4 mm, so a card edge riding on the
shroud ramps onto the blade and off again, and no edge is exposed for a
card to catch. To open, an MG90S servo in a pocket in the −Y tower slides
the blade 17.5° counter-clockwise along the shroud's inner surface. The
servo drives through an M2 pin on an 18 mm horn into a radial slot in the
blade's drive tab, which sits beside the shroud end at |Y| 46.5–48.5,
outside the card width. Gravity keeps the blade against the shroud
everywhere it travels. With the window open, a card at 208° slides out
under 0.47 g into the exit nip.

## 3.4 Feeder (entry at 50°)

Cards travel along their 63.5 mm edge. The hopper interior is 65 (radial)
by 91 (axial), and its floor is the plane of the entry fin, 46.7° from
horizontal. The deck slides against the gate under 0.73 g, so lid ballast
matters only for the last cards.

| Feature | r (mm) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Beam E, "card in the slot" | 81.5 | oblique, from the +Y tower (4 mm above the card plane) to the −Y tower (4 mm below). It crosses the plane of the card in the target slot near the centre, so that card blocks it completely. Neighbour cards are 9.5 mm away |
| Seated card outer edge | 85.5 | |
| Beam S, "card seated" | 86.8 | oblique like beam E. It is blocked only while a card still bridges the plate and the slot |
| Entry nip axis (Ø16 roller, 4 O-rings 10 × 3) | 96 | nearest point r 88.3; the deck plate starts at the nip (r 88.5) |
| Idler wheels | over the outer O-rings, Y ±27 | spring-loaded arm, pivot at r 107.75; pen springs bear on a `spring_bar` screwed across the pivot posts |
| Beam B (gate beam) | 106.5 | detector in the idler arm's boss, emitter in the plate |
| Gate lip | 118 | slotted clamp, feeler gauge, 0.45 mm nominal |
| Feed roller axis (Ø27, 4 O-rings 20 × 3.5) | 129.5 | crown 0.7 mm above the floor |
| Hopper | 118–183 | back wall to 186; lid hinge on the back wall |

The nip pushes the card until 10.5 mm before the hub. The card bridges
11 mm from the nip to the fin tips, then slides down the fin face under
0.73 g and stops on the hub tube. The firmware sets the fin 0.3° below the
card plane, so the card meets the fin's chamfered lead-in rather than its
end. The rollers are 64 mm long, so each N20 motor fits between the roller
end and the tower (Y 32.5–56.5). The next card is pre-staged 10 mm past the
gate. The gate geometry and thickness range are unchanged: t_max < gap <
2 t_min, adjustable 0.30–0.60 mm.

## 3.6 Towers, drive, base

* **Towers.** Two 12 mm plates at |Y| = 58–70, printed flat with the outer
  face down (213 × 198 mm, fits a 220 mm bed). Each has:
  * a 608ZZ bearing pocket;
  * a foot on the base;
  * a crossbar carrying the shroud tabs;
  * an arm under the feeder deck with two vertical M3 holes (the deck
    screws down into it);
  * a chute cheek with two horizontal holes (the chute screws to it from
    outside);
  * a 4 mm inner-face boss holding the beam E and beam S emitters (+Y) or
    detectors (−Y).

  The +Y tower also has a post at 12 o'clock for the index bracket and
  four counterbored holes for the motor bolts. The separate `motor_mount`
  (a hollow Ø50 × 28 standoff holding the coupler) sits on its outer face;
  four M3 × 40 bolts go from the tower's inner face through both into the
  NEMA 17. The −Y tower has the shutter servo pocket and horn recess.
* **Drive.** NEMA 17 → 5–8 mm flexible coupler → M8 threaded rod, cut to
  165 mm, clamped to the wheel with nyloc nuts. The rod turns in the
  bearings: wrap one layer of tape on the thread at each bearing seat so it
  does not rattle. Direct drive, 3200 µsteps per revolution, 0.11° per
  microstep.
* **Base.** Front and rear boxes (193 and 40 mm long, 210 wide, 45 tall)
  with bottom covers. The tower feet screw to the base top, so the axis is
  100 mm above it. The battery and perfboard go inside, and the panel is on
  the front face.

## 3.7 Printed parts

The full list, with quantities, sizes, orientation, settings and
post-processing, is `cad/print-settings.md`: **24 STL files, 30 printed
pieces** (2 × `roller_nip`, 2 × `idler_arm`, 4 × `idler_wheel`,
2 × `spring_bar`). Every STL rests on Z = 0 in its print orientation, and
`cad/check_printability.py` confirms that each one is a closed solid that
fits a 220 mm bed and prints without supports. Total ≈ 1.25 kg [EST]
(≈ 0.9 kg PETG, ≈ 0.35 kg PLA).

## 3.8 Tolerances and adjustment points

| Fit | Nominal | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Wheel position at the entry | ±0.7° (±1 mm at the mouth) | `cal home`, `cal entry` (default −0.3°), `cal exit` |
| Slot mouth | 7.6 mm at the tip, 1.16 at the hub | none; the funnel self-guides |
| Seated card edge to nearest fixed part | ≥ 1.3 mm (beam S line) | none |
| Card in the cage (axial) | 92 for 88.9 | none |
| Shroud to card edge | 3 mm | shim the shroud tabs if cards rub |
| Shutter blade to shroud | 0.3 mm | sand the blade's outer face |
| Gate gap | 0.45 | feeler gauge (the critical adjustment) |
| Idler wheels to roller O-rings | O-ring contact at the card plane | pen springs; add a washer for more force |
| Beam thresholds | auto | `cal beams` |
| Bearing pockets | 22.2 for 608ZZ | press fit |

Print variation is absorbed by the funnel-shaped slot mouths, the firmware
trims and the feeler-gauge gate. The only printed dimension that affects
randomness is the fin pitch. That is a division of the circle and cannot
drift.

## 3.9 Card protection

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Scratches | rolling contact in the nips; cards slide face-on over smooth fins; rounded gate lip; felt on the chute end wall and under the lid boss |
| Edge wear | the hub stop is a 2 mm PETG wall; in the lower half the edges ride on the shroud and on the tapered shutter; a felt strip inside the shroud is an optional upgrade |
| Bent corners | straight radial entry onto a chamfered fin; the wheel cannot turn while beam S or beam X sees a card half in a slot |
| Excessive pressure | nips about 2 N; lid about 0.6 N |
| Exposed faces | face down under the lid; between fins inside the cage; only the top card's back shows in the chute |
| Double feed | gate at 1.3–1.5 × card thickness; beam-B and beam-X block-time checks; end count |
| Cards flying out | shroud and shutter in the lower half; gravity and a 360°/s speed limit in the open upper half |
| Cards carried into the entry nip | the re-seating ramp on `shroud_B` pushes cards back from the shroud's 3 mm of play before the entry (§3.3) |

## 3.10 Things a builder may want to change

`entry_slot_ang` (steeper hopper), `exit_slot_ang` (steeper exit, longer
chute), `fin_t`, `n_slots` (keep `N_SLOTS_HW` in the firmware equal),
`roller_len` (the N20 must still fit inside the towers). After any change,
run `cad/check_clearance.sh`.
